1. GENEL

Soru: Does Spendoa work offline? Cevap: Yes. Spendoa works offline-first. Even
without an internet connection, you can view your past transactions and reports.
For security reasons, adding new transactions requires an internet connection.

Soru: Where is my data stored? Cevap: Your data is stored securely on encrypted
remote servers. Even if you change devices, you can access your data by logging
into your account.

Soru: What permissions does Spendoa require? Cevap: It only requests
Notification permission to send you reminders. Spendoa does not require camera,
contacts, or location permissions.

Soru: Does the app support widgets? Cevap: Yes. You can add the Spendoa widget
to your home screen to quickly check your account status and remaining limit
without opening the app.

2. HESAPLAR

Soru: Can I create multiple accounts? Cevap: Yes. You can create unlimited
Account, Credit Card, Bank Account, Cash, or Asset accounts.

Soru: Why does the data change when I switch accounts? Cevap: Each account in
Spendoa is independent. When you switch accounts, that account's currency,
statistics, and history are loaded.

Soru: Can I transfer money between accounts? Cevap: Yes. You can easily add
transfer transactions, such as transferring funds from your cash account to your
credit card or savings account.

Soru: Can I use different currencies? Cevap: Yes. You can set a different
currency for each account. During transfers, Spendoa fetches live exchange rates
and converts automatically.

Soru: How often are exchange rates updated? Cevap: Exchange rates used in
transfers are fetched in real-time from global markets as long as you have an
internet connection.

Soru: Can I customize the currency format? Cevap: Yes. You can customize
thousand separators (., , space), decimal separators, and the number of decimal
digits.

Soru: Why is the month start date different for each account? Cevap: Your salary
date or billing cycle may differ. For this reason, each account has its own
independent "Month Start" setting.

Soru: Can I use a shared account? Cevap: Spendoa is designed for individual use.
However, you can manage the same data with your partner if you both sign in
using the same Google account.

3. İŞLEMLER

Soru: How do I add installment purchases? Cevap: When adding an expense, select
"Installment Payment." Enter the total amount and the number of installments;
the app will automatically split the amount across months.

Soru: How can I track my debts and receivables? Cevap: Use the "Debts &
Receivables" module in the menu. Add a person to easily track how much you owe
or are owed.

Soru: Can I only make expenses from the account? Cevap: No. You can add Income,
Expense, or Transfer transactions using any Credit Card, Cash, or Asset account.

Soru: How should I record credit card payments? Cevap: Record them as a
"Transfer" transaction. Transfer money from your bank account to your Credit
Card account so your expenses are not counted twice.

Soru: How do I correct a wrong transaction? Cevap: Tap the transaction to open
its details screen. Use the "Edit" or "Delete" options to update it.

Soru: What happens if I exceed my account? Cevap: The app will not block you,
but it will warn you. Your account bar turns red to help you plan better for the
next month.

Soru: How do recurring transactions work? Cevap: Each account has its own
recurring income/expense rules. When the scheduled date arrives, the transaction
is added automatically.

4. GÜVENLİK

Soru: Can I lock the app? Cevap: Yes. You can enable Biometric Authentication
(Fingerprint/Face ID) in Settings. This feature is free to use.

Soru: What is amount masking? Cevap: Amount Masking hides your balances in
crowded environments so others cannot see them. This feature is free.

Soru: Is my data safe? Cevap: Yes. Your data is protected with industry-standard
encryption. No one, including the Spendoa team, can access your information.

Soru: Can I delete my account? Cevap: Yes. Go to Settings > Account > Delete My
Account to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

5. PREMIUM

Soru: What are the benefits of Premium? Cevap: Premium includes features like
Financial Score, Future Forecast, Advanced Reports, and Unlimited Accounts.
Basic accounting features remain free.

Soru: What is Lifetime? Cevap: Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Pay once and get
lifetime access to all Premium features.

Soru: Why do prices vary by country? Cevap: Our pricing is automatically
optimized based on income levels, inflation, and local currency purchasing
power.

Soru: What if the price seems high? Cevap: You can request a price review
through the Feedback section in the app or by emailing us.

Soru: I purchased Premium but features are not activated? Cevap: Tap "Restore
Purchases" in Settings. If the issue persists, please contact support.

Soru: How do I cancel my subscription? Cevap: Cancel via Google Play Store >
Subscriptions. Your benefits will continue until the end of the current billing
period.
